# Code of conduct

We want the Speko Gateway community to be welcoming, constructive, and safe.
Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good intent,
accept technical feedback gracefully, and focus criticism on ideas and code.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and the
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable in
project spaces or when representing the project publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently
exclude participants whose behavior is harmful to the community. Enforcement
decisions will consider context, impact, and repeated behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to `abat@speko.ai`. Reports will be
handled confidentially to the extent practical. Security vulnerabilities
should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
